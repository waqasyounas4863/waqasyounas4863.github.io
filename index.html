How It Works

The Exact Workflow Behind Every Article

No shortcuts and no guesswork. This is the same six-step process I follow on my own blog project — applied to yours.

1

Keyword Research

Niche keywords with informational intent are pulled through research and passed through strict filters before anything is written.

Strict filters applied to every keyword
2

Competitor Discovery

The keyword is searched on Google. I only target it when the sites already ranking are small and young — the kind a focused blog can realistically outrank.

Small, young ranking sites only
3

Traffic Checks

Candidate competitor sites are checked with traffic tools. Only viable competitors are kept — and their keywords are noted for the plan.

4

Top Pages Analysis

Each competitor’s top pages are analysed. Their proven keywords move into a Google Sheet — that sheet becomes the content plan.

5

Writing & On-Page SEO

The article is written against the top-ranking results on Google, answer-first — with direct answers, structured headings and FAQs — then optimised in WordPress with Rank Math.

Rank Math 80+ before publishing Answer-ready (AEO) structure
6

Indexing & Technical Checks

After publishing, every URL is inspected in Google Search Console and run through a live test to push indexing — and the technical foundation is checked alongside it: crawl and indexing, sitemaps, speed (especially on mobile) and HTTPS, using the admin-level settings I run on my own project.
